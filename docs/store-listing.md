# Store listing: Google Play (and later the App Store)

Text for Play Console → **Grow → Store presence → Main store listing**.
Arabic is the default language; add English as a translation.
⚠️ = the association must fill in or decide.

Limits: app name 30 characters, short description 80, full description 4000.
Play does not allow words like "best", "#1", "free", or prices and calls to
action in the name or short description.

## English

**App name** (10 characters)

Good Deeds

**Short description** (76 characters)

Volunteer events in Tripoli on a map. Join, log your hours, see your impact.

**Full description**

Good Deeds is the volunteer events app of the Educational Reform Association
(جمعية الإصلاح التربوي) in Tripoli, Libya.

Find the association's events on a map or in a list: clean-ups, tree
planting, charity kitchens, visits to the elderly and to orphanages, Qur'an
circles, tutoring and more. Pick one that suits you, read its terms,
and join.

At the event, a supervisor records when you start and finish. Then you
confirm your hours, or dispute them and the association reviews it.

Your confirmed hours become points across three pillars: spiritual, mental
and physical. As your points grow, so does your level.

What's inside:
• A map and a list of events in Tripoli
• Events for men, for women, and mixed
• Each event's terms, one at a time, before you join
• Attendance recorded by the supervisor, hours confirmed by you
• Your points, your level and your three pillars
• Propose a new event once you reach the level for it
• Report any problem to the association; the person you name is not told
• Notifications when hours wait for you, or an event's time or place changes
• Arabic and English

Your privacy:
• Sign in with your phone number and a code sent by SMS
• Your photo is seen only by people of your gender and the association
• No ads, no selling of data, no tracking
• Delete your account at any time from Settings

The sign-in screen also has a demo that works without an account, so you can
look around before you sign up.

## Screenshots to take

Play needs at least 2 phone screenshots (up to 8), 9:16 portrait, each side
between 320 and 3840 px; 1080 × 1920 or 1080 × 2400 works. Take one set with
the app in Arabic (for the Arabic listing) and one in English.

Use the demo accounts, so no real member's name or photo appears. ⚠️ Check
each one: no real phone numbers, no photos of real people.

1. **Map** (Map tab): Tripoli with several event pins.
   Caption: «فعاليات تطوعية قربك» / "Volunteer events near you".
2. **Event page**: حملة نظافة السراج, with the three-pillar bar and branches
   of faith. Caption: «اعرف الفعالية قبل أن تنضم» / "Know the event before you join".
3. **Terms carousel**: one term on screen, during joining.
   Caption: «شروط واضحة، واحداً بعد واحد» / "Clear terms, one at a time".
4. **Supervisor roster** (as خالد منصور): people with start/finish recorded.
   Caption: «المشرف يسجّل حضورك» / "Your supervisor records your attendance".
5. **Confirm hours** (as أحمد سالم, My activity → the recorded hours).
   Caption: «أكّد ساعاتك بنفسك» / "You confirm your own hours".
6. **Profile**: level, points and the three pillars.
   Caption: «ثلاثة أعمدة تُبنى فيك» / "Three pillars that grow with you".
7. **Events list** with the men / women / mixed labels.
   Caption: «فعاليات للرجال وللنساء ومختلطة» / "Events for men, women, or mixed".
8. *(optional)* **Report a problem** screen.
   Caption: «أبلغ الجمعية عن أي مشكلة» / "Tell the association about a problem".

Tips:

- The demo badge («نسخة تجريبية») shows on demo screens. ⚠️ Either keep it
  (honest, the demo is in the app) or take the screenshots signed in to a
  test account on a test server with made-up people.
- Before taking them, set the phone's clock to a clean time and full battery,
  or use an emulator.
- Tablet screenshots are needed only if you list the app for tablets.
